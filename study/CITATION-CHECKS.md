# Citations and scope

1. R. de Koster, T. Le-Duc and K. J. Roodbergen, 2007. DOI [10.1016/j.ejor.2006.07.009](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.ejor.2006.07.009). [Publisher PDF via Erasmus University](https://pure.eur.nl/ws/portalfiles/portal/46713708/DesignandControl_2007.pdf), section on routing and order batching, including proximity batching. Supports separating order grouping and route decisions and the example of grouping by picking location. The randomized hidden-ticket example is a proposed design, not a procedure observed in this warehouse review.
2. W. Wiesemann, D. Kuhn and B. Rustem, 2013. DOI [10.1287/moor.1120.0566](https://doi.org/10.1287/moor.1120.0566). [Full author paper](https://optimization-online.org/wp-content/uploads/2010/05/2610.pdf), §2.2 and Example 2.2. Supports a distinction between coupled and rectangular uncertainty sets. The source addresses transition probabilities in dynamical models; the dispatch calculation specializes affine utilities at a single decision stage. No claim of a new general robust-optimization principle.
